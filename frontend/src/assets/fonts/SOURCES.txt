RAG Serif and RAG Sans: Latin subsets of Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3 (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
Subsetting is a modification, and "Source" is a Reserved Font Name of the originals, so the subset families are
renamed in their name tables; copyright, trademark and licence records are unchanged. The licences are next to the
fonts, unchanged. How the files were made: docs/briefs/ui-visual-design.md section 4.1 and appendix A.
