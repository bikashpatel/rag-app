**What the app keeps, and for how long**

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| The account's confirmed email address and when it was confirmed | With the account |
| For "Continue with Google": Google's account identifier, the Google address when it was connected and whether Google had verified it | Until the user disconnects Google or the account is deleted |
| The version of the terms the user accepted, and when | With the account |
| A sign-up not yet confirmed: the address, the password (hashed), a hash of the browser's sign-up cookie, the page to open afterwards, the terms record and an approximate IP address | 7 days at most |
| An email address waiting for confirmation | 24 hours after the last confirmation email |
| Hashes of the links in emails and of the addresses they were sent to | A day after they are used or expire |
| The recipient and kind of each email the app sends | Until it is sent (then only a hash), the record 48 hours more |
| Hashes of addresses whose mail bounced or that reported a mail as spam | 30 days for a bounce; a spam report until the user confirms the address again |
| Account changes (sign-up, confirmations, password resets and changes, email changes, Google connections) with an approximate IP address | The IP address 90 days, the record 365 days or until the account is deleted |
| The previous email address after an email change (encrypted), so its owner can undo the change | 8 days |
| The usernames of deleted accounts (nothing else), so they are never given to someone else | Always |
| Sign-ins: approximate IP address, browser, operating system, device and the way of signing in | The newest 20 |
| Counters that limit repeated attempts (network prefixes and address hashes) | Their time window, at most a day |

**What the app never keeps**: the name, picture or tokens of a Google account (it doesn't ask for them), the results of the robot check, full IP addresses beyond the attempt counters, and the links in emails.

**Who processes it**

- **Resend** sends every email the app sends users (sign-up and email confirmations, password resets, security notices, receipts and plan reminders). It keeps account data in the United States and the content of messages for 30 days.
- **Cloudflare** runs the robot check (Turnstile) on sign-up, password reset and, after failed attempts, sign-in; it sees the visitor's IP address, TLS fingerprint and browser (User-Agent). Cloudflare also carries all traffic to the site.
- **Google** signs people in with "Continue with Google", asking only for the account identifier and email address.
- **Have I Been Pwned** checks new passwords against known breaches: only the first 5 characters of a hash of the password leave the app.

**Emails**: the app sends only what an account needs: confirmations, password resets and security notices (no opt-out), and receipts and plan reminders (each with a switch in Settings → Plan & billing). No newsletters, tracking pixels or click tracking.

**Export and deletion**: a user's data export includes their email, an address waiting for confirmation, the connected Google address and date, the terms record and the account changes. Deleting an account deletes these; billing records (payments and receipts) stay under the username [say why you keep them, and for how long].
