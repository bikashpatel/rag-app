## The service

AskNuvexusDoc is an assistant for your documents. You upload files (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, HTML, Markdown, text, CSV or JSON) and ask questions about them in a chat. Answers appear as they are written and cite the passages they used, so you can check them. Questions search your own documents and a shared knowledge base that we maintain. Depending on your plan, the assistant can also create files (PDF, Word, Excel, Markdown) and images, and work with your Gmail.

Answers, files and images are produced by AI models from Anthropic and OpenAI. They can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, even when they cite a source, and the same question can get different answers. Check anything important against the cited sources before you rely on it. The service doesn't give legal, medical, financial, tax or other professional advice and is no substitute for it.
