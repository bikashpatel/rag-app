# About us

Last updated: 1 November 2026

## What AskNuvexusDoc is

AskNuvexusDoc is an AI assistant for your own documents. Upload your contracts, internal reports, policies and handbooks, ask questions in plain language, and get clear answers with references to the exact passages behind them. It is made for professionals and small teams who need answers from their documents without searching or reading hundreds of pages.

## How it works

1. **Create an account and choose a plan.** Plans are priced in rupees for 30 days; see [Pricing](/pricing).
2. **Upload your documents**: PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, HTML, Markdown, text, CSV or JSON. AskNuvexusDoc reads each file, scanned PDF pages included, and indexes it for search.
3. **Ask questions in the chat.** Each answer appears as it is written and cites the passages it used, so you can check every claim. You can limit a question to the documents you choose.
4. **Go further on larger plans**: ask for a PDF, Word, Excel or Markdown file or an image, or work with your Gmail, all from the chat.

## Your privacy

Your documents and chats are private to your account; other users can't see them. To answer a question, AskNuvexusDoc sends it, with the most relevant passages from your documents, to the AI provider of the model you use (Anthropic or OpenAI). We don't sell your data, show advertising or use tracking cookies. Our [Privacy policy](/privacy) explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.
