# Shipping and delivery

Last updated: 1 November 2026

AskNuvexusDoc is an online service. Nothing is shipped, and there are no delivery charges.

## How you get access

- Your plan is delivered to your account online. Paid through Razorpay, it starts as soon as Razorpay confirms your payment, usually within a few minutes. After paying you come back to our site, which shows your plan, its dates and your receipt number.
- Paid directly by UPI: your plan starts when we confirm the payment, usually within a few hours during the day (Indian time). If it hasn't started within 24 hours, email admin@elarxsys.com with the UTR.
- A renewal, or a switch to another plan paid in advance, starts when your current paid time ends.
- Now and then a payment needs a check before the plan starts; the page then says so. We start your plan or refund the payment, so please don't pay again.
- If a plan paid through Razorpay hasn't started within 24 hours of paying, email admin@elarxsys.com with the date and amount of the payment and the payment ID from Razorpay's confirmation email.

## Receipts

Each payment gets a PDF receipt, emailed to your account's confirmed email address (if it has one) and always available in Settings → Plan & billing. For a payment through Razorpay, Razorpay also emails a payment confirmation to the address you give it.
