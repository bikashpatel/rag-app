# Contact us

Last updated: 1 November 2026

## How to reach us

Email is the quickest way: write to admin@elarxsys.com. We reply within 1 to 2 working days. Our phone number and postal address are below.

## What to include

- Your username or your account's email address. Never send us your password.
- What you were doing, what you expected and what happened, with the time and, if it helps, a screenshot.
- For a payment: the receipt number (Settings → Plan & billing), or the date and amount and Razorpay's payment ID, or, for a UPI payment, the UTR and the reference (AN-…).
- For a privacy request (a copy of your data, a correction, deleting your account): write from your account's email address, so we can tell the request is yours.

## Things you can do yourself

- **Forgotten password**: use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page (it needs a confirmed email on the account).
- **A copy of your data**: Settings → Plugins & data → Export my data.
- **Deleting chats and documents**: from the chat list, the documents panel, or Settings → Plugins & data → Delete all chats.
- **Deleting your account**: email us; the app has no button for it yet.
- **Refunds**: see our [Cancellation and refunds policy](/refunds).

## Complaints

If we haven't solved your problem, write to our Grievance Officer, whose details are at the end of our [Privacy policy](/privacy). The Grievance Officer acknowledges every complaint within 48 hours and resolves it within one month.

## Bank transfers

You can also pay for a plan by bank transfer (NEFT, RTGS or IMPS). Email us first with the plan you want, and we will reply with our bank details and the reference to use. Your plan starts once we have recorded the transfer, and you get a receipt as usual.
